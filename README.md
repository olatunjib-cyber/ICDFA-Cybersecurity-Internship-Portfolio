## 📖 About This Internship

This repository documents my practical work and learning journey throughout the **12-Month Ethical Hacking & Digital Forensic Internship Program at International Cybersecuirity & Digital Forensic Academy (ICDFA) **.

The internship is a structured, hands-on training program designed to develop practical cybersecurity skills through real-world laboratory exercises, technical projects, and security assessments. As I progress through the program, I continue to build knowledge and practical experience in both offensive and defensive cybersecurity by working on labs that simulate real-world environments and industry scenarios.

This repository serves as my personal portfolio, where I document the labs, projects, reports, and technical write-ups completed during the internship. Each lab includes the objectives, methodology, tools used, screenshots, findings, and lessons learned, providing a record of my continuous growth and hands-on experience.
